Are mental health days covered? ▾
Yes — mental health days are fully covered. NSU treats mental and physical health equally. You are not required to disclose the nature of your illness.
Can I use my sick days if my probation gets postponed? ▾
Yes — New employees can begin using paid sick leave beginning on the 90th day of their employment with the Company.

📋 How to Start an ERG

  • Gather 5–15 employees with a shared interest
  • Include at least one team leader as sponsor
  • Define purpose, member list & estimated costs
  • Submit via HR's Internal Approval system
  • Meet 1–4 times monthly; submit quarterly reports

💰 Budget & Support

  • $50 per person per month company-provided
  • Covers activity fees, equipment, meals & venues
  • ~$30,000 estimated annual total budget
  • Approval: HR Mgr → Division Head → CEO (>$1,000)
  • Additional costs require prior written approval

📜 Ground Rules

  • Rule 1: Clear purpose and defined goals
  • Rule 2: Meet regularly — 1–4 times per month
  • Rule 3: Quarterly reports with photos required
  • Rule 4: 2 inactive quarters = dissolution
  • Rule 5: Open to all NSU employees

🏓 Example Activities

  • Table Tennis — monthly at Factory 2 training room
  • Bowling — monthly outings at local lanes
  • Hiking & Outdoor — monthly adventure group
  • Cooking Club, Book Club, Language Exchange
  • Community volunteer and service days
